---
name: Food Hub launch boundaries
description: Why the first working version uses guest persistence and keeps provider-dependent services unavailable.
---
The initial working version is a guest cooking workspace, not a launched instructor marketplace.

**Why:** The approved 19-screen designs contained sample cooks, payments, delivery and verification, but no real providers, instructors, consented profiles or licensed launch imagery had been supplied. Persist useful cooking features without implying external services are live.

**How to apply:** Preserve clear sample/draft/unavailable distinctions until each service is truly integrated. Never convert saved lesson drafts into claims of reservations, paid orders or messages sent to cooks. Add proper managed authentication before cross-device accounts or sensitive information. Use owned/licensed photographs before public commercial launch.
